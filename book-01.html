Most people start in the wrong place. They open Instagram, look at the biggest pages, and decide the goal is simple: get as many followers as possible. Bigger number, bigger success.

Chapter 2 · Pick a Niche, Build a Theme

Most beginners think a theme is just a color palette. It is far more than that. Your theme is the sum of every choice that repeats across your posts: the type of clip you use, the way you cut it, the font and placement of your captions, the tone of your writing, even the length of your videos.

When these choices stay consistent, something powerful happens. A stranger scrolling their feed begins to recognize your work before they ever see your name.

Standout Ideas

Three things you won't unlearn.

The recognition test

Lay ten posts side by side, only three of them yours. If a follower can't pick out your three without reading a handle, your theme is invisible and no one will remember you tomorrow.

The three-question niche filter

Does it attract Tier 1 advertiser money? Is there an endless supply of content to pull from? Can you stand making it daily for a year? Fail one, and you keep looking.
