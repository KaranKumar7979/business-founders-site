Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about the books, how buying works, where we stand on refunds, and the editor service. If your question is not here, email hello@thebusinessfounders.com and a real person will read it.

The books

What do I actually get?

Four e-books called The Founder Series: How to Grow a Theme Page From Scratch, Monetizing Your Existing Theme Page, How to Launch a Business With AI, and Growing Your Business With AI. You can buy any single book on its own or take all four together.

What format are they in?

Every book is a PDF, so it opens on a phone, tablet, laptop or e-reader without installing anything. We do not advertise a page count. The free opening chapter shows you the writing before you spend money.

Which book should I start with?

The titles are literal, so pick the one that matches where you are right now. If you are not sure, read the free opening chapter of any book first and judge it yourself.

Do I need an existing theme page to get value from them?

No. How to Grow a Theme Page From Scratch is the one written for starting with nothing, and Monetizing Your Existing Theme Page is the one written for a page that already has an audience. The two AI books are about launching and growing a business rather than a theme page.

Buying and delivery

How much do the books cost?

Single books are $14 during launch, rising to $19. All four are $39 during launch, rising to $49. The two-book bundles are $24, rising to $29.

How does delivery work?

Checkout runs on Gumroad, and your PDF is ready to download as soon as the payment goes through. Gumroad also sends a receipt by email with a link back to your download.

My download link did not work. Now what?

Check the receipt email from Gumroad first, since it links back to your download. If that still will not open, email hello@thebusinessfounders.com and we will get the file to you.

Which payment methods do you take, and is checkout secure?

Gumroad is the merchant of record, so it processes the payment and handles sales tax. The payment methods available are the ones Gumroad shows at checkout for your country. Card details are entered on Gumroad's side. This site never sees or stores them.

Refunds and trying before you buy

Can I get a refund?

No. Digital downloads here are final sale and there is no money-back guarantee. Once the file has downloaded it cannot be given back, so please use the free chapters before you buy.

That feels risky. How do I know the books are worth it?

Read the writing before you pay anything. The opening chapter of every book is free, and it is there so you can judge the quality for yourself while it still costs you nothing.

How do I get the free chapters?

Each book page offers that book's first chapter as a direct PDF download with no email required. There is also a four-chapter sampler through a free Gumroad product, and that one does ask for an email address.

What if something genuinely goes wrong with my order?

Email hello@thebusinessfounders.com and tell us what happened. That is the fastest way to get it fixed. Gumroad also runs its own dispute process for buyers, and that sits outside our control.

Editors and getting in touch

Can you find me an editor?

Yes, that is what the Hire an Editor page is for. You leave your name, Instagram handle, email and optionally your Telegram, then we get in touch to talk through what you need.

Do I pay for an editor through this site?

No. The form is an enquiry only, nothing is charged on the site, and anything about scope or cost is agreed after we have spoken.

I am an editor. Can I apply to work with you?

Not yet. Editor applications are not open, and we would rather say that plainly than collect applications we cannot act on.

How do I reach a human?

Email hello@thebusinessfounders.com or message @businessfounders_ on Instagram. This is a small operation, so replies are not instant, but every message is read.
