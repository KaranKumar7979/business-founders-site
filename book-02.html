Chapter 3 · Paid Shoutouts and Promotions

For most theme pages, this is the first money that ever arrives. Someone with a page, a product, an app, or a service pays you to put them in front of your audience. It is simple, it is quick, and it requires nothing from you except the audience you already have and a post you already know how to make.

Be specific about the deliverable, because vagueness is where disputes come from. Decide how long the post stays up, whether they supply the content or you make it, whether they get a link, and how many revisions are included. Write those terms down in the message thread before any money moves.

Chapter 5 · Brand Deals

The assumption that brands only work with huge accounts is out of date. Smaller pages with engaged, well-defined audiences get approached regularly, because a business selling accounting software would rather reach twenty thousand people who care about business than a million who do not.

Brands deal with a great many creators who miss deadlines, ignore the brief, and disappear after payment. Being straightforward and reliable is a genuine competitive advantage, and it is entirely within your control. That is where smaller pages win deals they should not have won on numbers alone.

Chapter 10 · Closing Deals and Not Getting Scammed

Page owners are targets. You have an audience, you are visibly open to being paid, and you are often young and new to this. Fake brand offers aimed at smaller creators are one of the most common frauds on the platform, and some of them are after your account rather than your money.

Two things you never do. You never pay to receive an opportunity, because real brands do not ask creators for money, for shipping, or for samples. And you never hand over a login, a password, or a verification code to anyone, for any reason, no matter how official the request looks.

Standout Ideas

Three things you won't unlearn.

The pricing test

Quote a number you'd be pleased with, without apologizing, and watch. If everyone says yes instantly, you're too cheap: raise it on the next enquiry. A few rounds teaches you your real rate faster than any guide.

Two rules that never bend

Get paid in full before you post for strangers. And never hand over a login, password, or verification code, no matter how official the request looks. That's how pages get stolen.
