Chapter 1 · You Do Not Need a Million Followers

The most common reason people never earn from a page they have already built is that they are waiting. Waiting to look big enough, to feel professional enough, to hit some number they picked because it sounded serious. Meanwhile pages a third of their size are getting paid.

The waiting is not caution, it is a misunderstanding of what buyers are actually buying. Nobody pays for your follower count. They pay for access to people who will act, which means people who watch, trust you, and click. Those are different things, and they come apart constantly. A page can be enormous and nearly worthless to an advertiser, and a page can be small and genuinely valuable. Once you understand what determines that, you stop waiting.

Standout Ideas

Three things you won't unlearn.

The pricing test

Quote a number you'd be pleased with, without apologizing, and watch. If everyone says yes instantly, you're too cheap: raise it on the next enquiry. A few rounds teaches you your real rate faster than any guide.

Two rules that never bend

Get paid in full before you post for strangers. And never hand over a login, password, or verification code, no matter how official the request looks. That's how pages get stolen.

Start where there's no gatekeeper

Why a small page should begin with affiliates and its own product (no approval needed, best margins) and let shoutouts and brand deals arrive as it grows. The opposite of what most people assume.
