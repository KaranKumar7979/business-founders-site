The Mission

Built From Zero.

Business Founders started with a simple frustration: most "business content" online is recycled noise. Motivational quotes with no substance. Courses that teach nothing. We wanted to build something different: content and products with real standards.

Today, @businessfounders_ reaches more than 200,000 entrepreneurs. Every e-book we publish and every editor we bring on is held to one question: would we pay for this ourselves?

If the answer isn't an immediate yes, it doesn't ship. That's the standard. That's why founders trust us.
